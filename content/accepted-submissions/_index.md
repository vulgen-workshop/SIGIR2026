The following extended abstracts have been accepted to VulGen'26 and will be presented in person at the workshop. These presentations will be part of the workshop's discussions and activities, contributing to our exploration of vulnerabilities in generative systems for information retrieval.

- LLM-Judge-as-a-System: The Co-adaptation Spiral  
  Laura Dietz

- EnCoNeSyRAG: A Neuro-Symbolic Framework for Engineered Agency and Bidirectional Coordination in Lightweight RAG  
  Dilana Mathugama and Dilum De Silva

- Secure Coding Drift in LLM-Assisted Post-Quantum Cryptography Development: A Gamified Fix  
  Shakya Rathnaikage, C.P. Wijesiriwardana, S.M. Vidanagamachchi and Nalin Arachchilage

- The Audit Gap: Organizational Vulnerabilities of Generative AI Adoption  
  Adam Roegiest

- Understanding and Debugging Failures in N-Gram-Based GR  
  Richard Takacs, Adrian Bracher and Svitlana Vakulenko

- How Long Reasoning Chains Influence LLMs' Judgment of Answer Factuality  
  Minzhu Tu, Shiyu Ni and Keping Bi

- The Effect of Multi-Lingual and Keyword Adversarial Injection on LLM Relevance Judgement  
  Nguyen Khoi Vo, Mark Sanderson, Oleg Zendel and Tuong Duy Duong

- When a Bot Cries Wolf: Exploring Voice AI Misuse through an Emergency Services Demo  
  Qizhou Wang, Hanxun Huang, Sarah Erfani and Christopher Leckie
