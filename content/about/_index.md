The **VulGen: International Workshop on Vulnerabilities in Generative Systems for Information Retrieval** aims to bring together researchers from information retrieval, cyber security, machine learning, human-computer interaction, and related fields, to foster cross-disciplinary collaboration and advance understanding of vulnerabilities in generative systems used for IR.

Generative systems are rapidly transforming both academic research and industrial practices. These systems are increasingly integrated into information access and information retrieval (IR) tasks and continue to evolve at a substantial pace. Integrating these models into daily workflows exposes critical vulnerabilities, including adversarial attacks, inherent biases, and negative impacts on user behavior, which can lead to suboptimal or even detrimental outcomes.

Topics of interest include (but are not limited to):
 - Adversarial attacks on generative IR systems
 - Biases and fairness issues in generative models for IR
 - Robustness and reliability of generative retrieval systems
 - Security and privacy vulnerabilities in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
 - Prompt injection and manipulation of generative IR systems
 - Misinformation and hallucination in generative search
 - User behavior and trust in generative IR systems
 - Evaluation methodologies for assessing vulnerabilities in generative IR
 - Defensive strategies and mitigation techniques

The VulGen workshop brings together the IR community and related disciplines (e.g., cyber security) to map this evolving landscape. Through a full day of structured discussion and engagement, we aim to synthesize the current state of research and identify new avenues for investigation.
